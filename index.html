01 /ABOUT

Curiosity as a starting point.

I am a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at Zhejiang University. My work connects diffusion and flow-based methods with language generation. I am especially interested in how generative systems can follow formal constraints while remaining flexible and useful.

Outside research, I enjoy photography, fitness, reading about people and ways of living, and romantic rock music. I like following my curiosity and sharing what I learn.
