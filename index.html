About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at Zhejiang University. My research focuses on diffusion language models and generative modeling.

I currently study decoding order in discrete diffusion language models and structured output in continuous diffusion language models. Earlier, I explored reinforcement learning for diffusion- and flow-based action policies.

I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2025. I also have experience with diffusion and flow matching methods for inverse problems.
